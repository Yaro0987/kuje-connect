Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 1, 2026

Kuje Connect ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services. By accessing or using Kuje Connect, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Data: When you register an account, we collect your full name, email address, phone number, business name, business address, and any other information you voluntarily provide in your profile.
  • Business Information: Business owners may provide additional details such as business description, operating hours, product or service listings, photographs, pricing information, and social media links.
  • Payment Information: If you subscribe to a paid plan, we collect billing details necessary to process transactions. Payment card information is processed by our secure third-party payment processors and is never stored on our servers.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect certain information when you visit our platform, including your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring URLs, and general location information (city/region level).
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: We use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to track activity on our platform, store preferences, and improve your experience. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences.
  • Communications: When you contact our support team, send messages to business owners, or leave reviews, we collect the content of those communications along with metadata such as timestamps.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your account and provide you with access to our services.
  • To display your business listing to potential customers searching for products and services in Kuje, FCT.
  • To process payments and manage your subscription plan.
  • To communicate with you about account updates, security alerts, and promotional offers.
  • To personalize your experience and deliver content and product offerings relevant to your interests.
  • To improve our website, services, and user experience through analytics and aggregated data.
  • To monitor and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats on our platform.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms of service.
  • To send you newsletters and marketing communications (with your consent, which you may withdraw at any time).

3. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • Public Business Listings: Information you provide for your business listing (business name, description, address, phone number, photos, and operating hours) is displayed publicly on our platform so that customers can find and contact you.
  • Service Providers: We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our platform, including hosting providers, payment processors, analytics services, and email delivery services. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid requests by public authorities, such as a court order or government agency request.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or a portion of our assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you via email and/or a prominent notice on our platform of any change in ownership or use of your personal information.
  • With Your Consent: We may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit using SSL/TLS technology.
  • Encrypted storage of sensitive data at rest.
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments.
  • Access controls and authentication mechanisms to limit unauthorized access.
  • Employee training on data protection and privacy practices.

While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but will promptly notify affected users in the event of a data breach as required by applicable law.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following types of cookies on our platform:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the platform to function properly, including session management and security features.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our platform, including pages visited and time spent. We use this data to improve our services.
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings to provide a more personalized experience.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign performance. These are only set with your consent.

You can manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of our platform.

6. Third-Party Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party websites, including social media platforms and business websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of these external sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party sites you visit. The inclusion of any link does not imply endorsement by Kuje Connect.

7. Children's Privacy

Kuje Connect is not intended for use by children under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 16, we will take steps to delete that information promptly. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@kujeconnect.com.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information. Your continued use of Kuje Connect after any changes to this policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
