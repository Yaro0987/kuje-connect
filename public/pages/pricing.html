Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our pricing

Can I switch plans at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. When you upgrade, the new features will be activated immediately and you will be charged the prorated difference. When you downgrade, the change will take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.
Is there a free trial for paid plans?
Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for both the Business and Enterprise plans. You can explore all the features of your chosen plan without any commitment. At the end of the trial, you can decide to subscribe or continue with our free plan.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept bank transfers, debit card payments, and USSD transfers. All payments are processed securely. We also support recurring payments through selected Nigerian banks to make your subscription seamless.
Can I cancel my subscription?
Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your account settings. Your paid features will remain active until the end of your current billing period. After that, your account will revert to the free plan with no data loss.
Are there discounts for annual billing?
Yes! When you choose annual billing, you get a 20% discount on both the Business and Enterprise plans. That means you pay ₦48,000/year instead of ₦60,000 for the Business Plan, and ₦144,000/year instead of ₦180,000 for the Enterprise Plan.
